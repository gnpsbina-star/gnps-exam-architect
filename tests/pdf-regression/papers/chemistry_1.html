29. Case Study 1: Clean Electrochemical Energy – The Fuel Cell
Conventional methods of generating electrical energy via the combustion of fossil fuels (coal, petroleum) operate with an overall efficiency of roughly $35\text{--}40\%$, producing significant greenhouse emissions. In contrast, Galvanic cells directly convert the chemical energy of a redox reaction into electrical work. Devices designed to continuously convert the energy of combustion of fuels such as hydrogen, methane, or carbon monoxide directly into electrical energy without an intermediate thermal cycle are known as fuel cells.
