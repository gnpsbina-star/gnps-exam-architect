Electrochemical galvanic cells transform Gibbs energy of spontaneous chemical reactions directly into electrical energy. Conventional thermal plants burn fossil fuels to generate steam and rotate turbines, operating with a modest efficiency of ~40% and releasing significant atmospheric pollutants. Fuel cells represent advanced galvanic cells that run on continuously supplied fluid fuels such as hydrogen, methane, or methanol. In the hydrogen-oxygen fuel cell used in the Apollo space programme, hydrogen and oxygen are bubbled through porous carbon electrodes into concentrated aqueous potassium hydroxide (KOH) electrolyte. The cell produces drinking water for astronauts while maintaining ~70% thermodynamic efficiency.

30. Case-Based Question 2: Read the passage and study the structures below to answer the questions that follow:
Carbohydrates serve as structural scaffolds and energy reservoirs in living organisms. D-glucose, an aldohexose, exists predominantly in cyclic pyranose forms produced by intramolecular hemiacetal formation between the −CHO group at C1 and the −OH group at C5. This generates two stereoisomeric cyclic forms: α-D-glucopyranose and β-D-glucopyranose, which differ only in the spatial configuration of the hydroxyl group at C1. Such diastereomers differing only at C1 are termed anomers. The open-chain form exists in minute amounts (~0.02%) in aqueous solution, which explains why glucose fails to undergo certain characteristic reactions of free aldehydes (e.g., Schiff's test or 2,4-DNP adduct formation).
